ALL THREE MUST PASS

Retention is measured among Standard/Preferred agents active at day 30, excluding confirmed misconduct removals. The 90% threshold is a pilot assumption, not an established Ethos standard. Early persistency comparisons require sufficiently mature cohorts.

90-day implementation

Days 1–30 · Contain and correct. Apply exposure restrictions immediately; protect agents who already meet Watch graduation criteria. Verify billing incidents, correct clawbacks, share adjusted data, prioritize payday-aligned billing, and establish the day-30 baseline.

Days 31–60 · Enforce and rehabilitate. Apply the full ladder, repayment schedules, affordability coaching, replacement investigations, and persistency-linked manager incentives.

Days 61–90 · Decide to scale. Offer Preferred terms to agents with sufficient pre-existing performance history, evaluate the three gates, and pause new-recruit advances if any fail.

Decision trade-offs

Ethos prioritizes reducing expected unrecoverable advance exposure, protecting customers, and retaining agents who write durable policies.

Willing to lose: near-term volume, recruitment speed, agents reliant on unconditional advances, and potentially Summit if it rejects sustainable terms.

Long-term North Star: net retained commission contribution from policies surviving to month 13, after unrecovered advance losses.

90-day leading measures: new unsecured exposure, three-premium persistency, productive-agent retention, affordability and billing-failure guardrails.

Interpretation & governance

Credibility adjustment: below 20 eligible policies, blend observed lapse rate with comparable peers. This simulator weights the observed rate by policies ÷ 20 (15% at 3 policies, 100% at 20), so there is no jump at the threshold. An illustrative minimum-evidence safeguard (not an explicit written-case threshold) limits quality downgrades to agents with at least 10 eligible policies. The quality trigger applies only from 10 eligible policies and only when the adjusted rate is above benchmark. The weighting is illustrative and would be calibrated on historical cohorts.

Persistency benchmark: for simplicity the three-premium persistency benchmark is set to 1 minus the peer lapse benchmark (85% when lapses benchmark at 15%); in production it would be measured directly on comparable cohorts.

Billing fairness: select Disputed (provisionally exclude while investigating), Verified Ethos-caused (exclude and reverse clawbacks/remediate), or Not Ethos-caused (include in scoring; no Ethos-error reversal). This attribution control is simulated; production decisions require verified billing logs.

Manager separation: evaluate manager's personal policies independently from team performance. Recover personal/inherited debt from earned overrides on a fixed schedule.

Advance vs. income: restrictions change the timing of otherwise earned commissions, not a guarantee that lapsed-policy commissions are earned.

Simulation scope: Current tier is a starting input; the displayed tier is a recommended single-period decision, not a saved operational change. Cure-window counts represent verified historical review outcomes, not an automatic award based on current-period inputs.

Source-of-truth controls: finance systems verify debt, billing logs verify incidents, investigation staff confirm misconduct, and rule changes are versioned and auditable.

Assumption: advance and override terms can be changed with contractual notice.
